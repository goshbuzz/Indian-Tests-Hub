# Coverage benchmark — 30 October 2026

## Our baseline

Run `npx tsx scripts/coverage-report.mts` to reproduce current counts. As checked on 30 October 2026, the hub listed 230 catalogue entries, 15 patterns marked verified, and 17 entries with a linked cycle-specific official source (including two descriptive or counselling entries without representative mocks). Unsourced historical verified flags are treated as unverified until audited. It contained 384 bank items with 384 distinct normalized question texts, and 22 distinct checked official paper links available across six exam entries. The 32,613 per-exam question instances count the same shared questions repeatedly; it is **not** the number of unique questions. A verified pattern does not mean its practice bank, eligibility, current cycle or other details are verified.

`npx tsx scripts/verify-banks.mts` checks section shortfalls; it exits nonzero while gaps remain. Many mocks are shorter than the official pattern or unavailable. The count of verified flags is not a substitute for auditing the remaining families and recording their sources.

## Competitors' published claims

- [Testbook](https://testbook.com/) advertises access to more than 50,000 mock tests and offers chapter, sectional, full and previous-year tests. Its listing also distinguishes free tests from the total. This is its own claim, not an independently audited count.
- [Adda247 Test Prime](https://www.adda247.com/product-testseries/79075/test-prime) advertises coverage of 800+ exams and 150,000+ mocks in a subscription. The same page also contains other counts, so these are inconsistent self-reported marketing figures rather than audited numbers.

The products, access tiers, time of measurement and definitions differ. Exam entries, unique questions, full mocks, paper links and per-exam instances are **not interchangeable**. We cannot claim the hub has more content than either competitor based on these figures.

## Priorities for closing the gap

1. Audit existing verified flags against current official bulletins; record source, cycle and date. Separate papers, accommodations and mixed question types rather than forcing every exam into one MCQ template.
2. Expand original, reviewed subject-specific question banks and track unique text, answer validity, topic and difficulty; do not count shared instances as new questions.
3. Add official previous-paper links and alerts only after checking the conducting body's source. Preserve expiry dates and update history; do not imply a feed is live when it is manually curated.
4. Improve discovery, per-topic practice, explanations and accessibility while making missing coverage visible. Do not copy competitor-owned content.
